Features

Built for painters and decorators

Show clients how a finish will look, and agree on it, before the first coat of primer goes on.

🎨

Colour wheel and hex codes

Pick any colour from the wheel, or type an exact #HEX code from a manufacturer's chart. The walls update instantly.

📜

Five designer wallpapers

Each pattern is drawn in your browser, so there are no image files to download and nothing to break. Pages load quickly and the patterns stay sharp up close.

⭐

Choose which wall to change

Style the accent wall on its own, such as a bold wallpaper with calm paint around it, or apply a finish to all walls at once.

☀️

Daylight, sunset and night

See how a colour changes through the day: bright daylight through the windows, warm low sunlight at golden hour, and soft evening light from the ceiling spots and cove lighting.

✨

Paint sheen levels

Switch between flat/matte, eggshell/satin and semi-gloss to see how much light each finish reflects.

🪵

Flooring options

Check how your wall finish pairs with white oak, dark walnut, Carrara marble or polished concrete.

🪑

Empty or furnished

The room starts empty so you can inspect the walls. One click adds a sofa, coffee table, armchair and lamp to show the finish in context.

📐

Room measurements

Show dimension lines in the room: 7 m wide, 8 m deep and 3 m high. Handy when estimating paint or wallpaper quantities.

📦

Import your own model

Drag in a .glb or .gltf file exported from SketchUp, Revit, Blender or 3ds Max to view a client's own room.

Controls

How to move around

Walk, look and interact on a desktop browser, a phone or tablet, or a VR headset.

Platform Move Look around Interact
💻 Desktop & Laptop W A S D or Arrow Keys Click and drag with the mouse Click the menu, colour pickers or floor rings
🥽 WebXR Headset Left controller: point and release to teleport Turn your head Right controller: aim the laser pointer and squeeze the trigger
📱 Mobile & Tablet Use the viewpoint buttons or tap a floor ring Drag with your finger Tap the on-screen menu and colour swatches
Device support

Works with VR headsets

Any headset with a WebXR-capable browser should work. There's nothing to install: open the page and enter VR.

🥽

Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S & Pro

Full head tracking and controller support in the Meta Quest Browser.

👓

Apple Vision Pro

WebXR support in Safari. You may need to enable it in the browser's feature flags.

⚡

Pico 4 & Neo 3

WebXR rendering and controller teleporting in the Pico Browser.

🖥️

HTC Vive & PCVR

WebXR in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a PC, connected through SteamVR.
